How to order souvla for a Sunday: cuts, quantities and timing

A butcher's guide to Cypriot souvla: which cut, how much per person, how far ahead to order and what to ask for at the counter.

Which cut?

For pork souvla, ask for shoulder (spala) or neck. Both have enough fat to stay juicy over charcoal; loin dries out. For lamb, shoulder again, or a mix of shoulder and leg if you want leaner pieces on the same skewer.

How much per person?

Plan on 350–400g of raw meat per adult, a little more if souvla is the only main. For a table of eight, that's 3kg — we'll cut it into 5–6cm cubes so each piece cooks evenly.

When to order

Call before 18:00 the day before, or any time on the app once it launches. Give us the name and pick-up time and it will be weighed, bagged and waiting at the counter.

What to ask for

Ask for the meat unsalted — salt it yourself an hour before cooking. Take a bag of our own charcoal and a lemon or two from the fruit market; everything else is in your kitchen already.
